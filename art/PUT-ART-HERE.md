# Art — done

All 25 monsters in this module now have art in this folder as `<slug>.png`
(see `monster-overhaul-art-manifest.md` in the module root for slugs and
source pages), and every actor references `modules/valdoria-overhaul/art/<slug>.png`
for both portrait and token.

**12 illustrations were extracted from Jason's own PDF copy of
*The Monster Overhaul* on 2026-10-30:** Baboon, Hive Insect, Fairy, Tiger,
Alicanto, Telluric Goat, Giant Crab, Ancient Arthropod, Mantis Shrimp,
Predatory Snail, Necromancer, Nightmare Beast.

**13 illustrations were generated in the book's own style on 2026-10-30 at
Jason's request**, because those monsters have no distinct illustration in
the book — their entry pages carry no image, another monster's art, a generic
map, or a chapter plate: Crocodile, Hippopotamus, Hyena, Lion, Boar, Dryad,
Giant Snake, Alpine Spectre, Panther, Roperite, Remora, Sea Serpent,
Leech of Paradise. Each was generated from the monster's description in its
book entry, using the book's Tiger illustration as the style reference
(black ink line work, halftone dot shading, plain off-white background).
